# Privacy and feedback

## What stays on your PC

- Your savegame. The Farm Manager reads it and never changes it.
- Your `sanctum` folder: your farm's memory, in plain text.
- The cards, your answers and the menu page's figures.

The Farm Manager's own scripts make no network connection.

## What does not stay on your PC

- **Your conversation with Cyrus.** Claude Code sends it to the AI model you have set it up
  with. That includes farm figures Cyrus read during the session.
- **Issue reports you approve.** See below.

## Reporting a problem or asking for a feature

At closeout Cyrus asks two things:

1. Whether anything logged this session looks like a real product bug worth sending to the
   project's issue tracker.
2. Whether there is anything you would like added or changed.

Before you are shown a draft, the text is scanned, and you are told what was checked:
usernames, home folder paths and author names. It is not a promise that nothing personal
remains, so read the draft.

You then see the exact title and text of each report, one at a time. For each you can
approve it, edit it, put it off, or drop it. There is no "approve all", and saying nothing
is never taken as yes.

Approved reports go to
[github.com/Hidden-History/FS25-AI-Farm-Manager/issues](https://github.com/Hidden-History/FS25-AI-Farm-Manager/issues).
That page is public.

You can also open an issue there yourself at any time.

## What to include in a report

- The version (the game's mod list shows it).
- What you saw and what you expected.
- The "as of" time on the tab, if the report is about a number.

Leave out your farm's name, your cash figures and your Windows username unless they matter
to the problem.
